05 Questions

Good questions, short answers.

How does Sign → Text work?+

Use your webcam or upload an image or video. Signrest validates the input, extracts hand features, predicts the sign with the AI model and shows the word with a confidence score.

Why do I need an OTP?+

A one-time code is emailed to you when you sign up, log in or reset your password. It confirms the account really belongs to you.

Can I review my past translations?+

Yes. Every translation is saved to your history, where you can review it or delete it whenever you want.

Is there an admin side?+

Yes. Admins have a dashboard for users, feedback, quizzes, sign materials and AI model accuracy logs.
