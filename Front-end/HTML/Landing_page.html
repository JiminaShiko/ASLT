    Trust & safety

    Your account stays yours

    Email OTP

    One-time codes expire quickly and are required to sign up, sign in and reset a password.

    Hashed passwords

    Passwords are stored as hashes, and sessions use refresh tokens that can be revoked.

    Login history

    Each sign-in is logged with its status, time and device so unusual activity is easy to spot.
